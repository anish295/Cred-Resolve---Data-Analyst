Confirmed Issues

A — Duplicate Payment References
FACT

Detection Method

GROUP BY payment_reference, COUNT(*) > 1.
Cross-checked payment_status distribution within duplicate groups.

Business Impact

₹19.16 Cr

14.3% over-count in raw recovery figure.
4,678 duplicate payment_reference values across 25,500 raw rows.

Treatment Applied

Keep first SUCCESS per reference; else keep earliest record.
Clean table: 20,822 rows (payment_id remains unique key).
Audit log: 4,678 rows removed with reason tag.

B — Agent Identity Fragmentation
FACT

Detection Method

GROUP BY employee_code, COUNT(DISTINCT agent_id).
Found employees with up to N different agent_ids across vendors.

Business Impact

~27×

Agent count inflated from 1,099 real employees to ~30,000 rows.
Per-agent productivity metrics are split across IDs — rankings unreliable.

Treatment Applied

Canonical agents table: one row per employee_code (most recent updated_at wins).
All downstream metrics join to canonical employee_code.

C — Disposition Code Schema Mismatch
FACT

Detection Method

Cross-tab of disposition_code × disposition_version.
Found PROMISE_TO_PAY (legacy) and PTP (v1/v2) for identical event types.

Business Impact

~50%

PTP rate appears to drop by ~50% in raw data when schema migrates from legacy to v1/v2.
This is a false signal — no real change in borrower behaviour.

Treatment Applied

PROMISE_TO_PAY → PTP (standardised).
disposition_std column added in staging layer.
All metric calculations use disposition_std, not raw code.

D — Timezone Mixing in Call Records
FACT

Detection Method

For each call, applied correct UTC offset per timezone (UTC=0, IST=+5:30, Dubai=+4).
Compared local-hour classification against UTC-corrected hour for DNC window (8AM–8PM).
60,086 of 90,079 clean calls have a different hour once corrected.

Business Impact

~24,967

Calls misclassified within DNC window (8AM–8PM local) — compliance checks unreliable.
Best-time-to-call models trained on uncorrected data produce biased schedules.

Treatment Applied

UTC offset annotated in staging (UTC=0, IST=+5.5, Dubai=+4).
call_hour_utc_corrected added in clean layer.
All calling-time analyses use corrected hour.

Strong Evidence Issues

E — Denominator Shrinkage (Survivorship Bias)
STRONG EVIDENCE

Detection Method

Tracked ACTIVE account count month-by-month using account_status_history.
Compared recovery rate with stable (all accounts) vs shrinking (active-only) denominator.

Business Impact

Unknown

Recovery RATE may be overstated if closed accounts leave the denominator.
Rate improves mechanically even with flat ₹ collected.

Treatment Applied

golden_account_snapshot reconstructs end-of-month active population.
All rate metrics computed on stable denominator (outstanding_amount).
Both rate definitions reported for comparison.

F — Attribution Window Errors
STRONG EVIDENCE

Detection Method

Joined payments to calls on account_id; filtered call_at > payment_at.
Significant share of payments have calls recorded AFTER the payment event.

Business Impact

Campaign ROI

Campaigns receive credit for payments they did not cause.
High-performing campaigns may be receiving false attribution.

Treatment Applied

Channel conversion metric enforces: payment event_at MUST be > touchpoint event_at.
7-day attribution window with mandatory temporal ordering.
